Problem Statement :-

Sum Of Lower Right Triangle in DT (Daily Test) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

N = int(input().strip())

matrix = [list(map(int , input().strip().split())) for i in range(0 , N)]

lower_right_triangle_sum = sum(matrix[i][j] for i in range(0 , N) for j in range((N - i - 1) , N))

print(lower_right_triangle_sum)
