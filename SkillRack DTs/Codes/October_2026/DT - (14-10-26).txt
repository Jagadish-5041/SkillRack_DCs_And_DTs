Problem Statement :-

Maximum - First , Last And Middle in DT (Daily Test) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

N = int(input().strip())

values_list = list(map(int , input().strip().split()))

maximum_value = max(values_list[0] , values_list[(N // 2)] , values_list[(N - 1)])

print(maximum_value)
