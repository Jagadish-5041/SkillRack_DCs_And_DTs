Problem Statement :-

Average - Except Largest And Smallest in DT (Daily Test) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

N = int(input().strip())

values_list = list(map(int , input().strip().split()))

minimum_value = min(values_list)

maximum_value = max(values_list)

sum_value = sum(values_list)

sum_value_except_minimum_and_maximum_value = (sum_value - minimum_value - maximum_value)

average_value_except_minimum_and_maximum_value = (sum_value_except_minimum_and_maximum_value / (N - 2))

print("{:.2f}".format(average_value_except_minimum_and_maximum_value))
