Problem Statement :-

Lower Left Triangle - Reverse Diagonally in DT (Daily Test) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

N = int(input().strip())

matrix = [list(map(int , input().strip().split())) for i in range(0 , N)]

[print(*[(matrix[(N - j - 1)][(N - i - 1)]) if(j <= i) else (matrix[i][j]) for j in range(0 , N)]) for i in range(0 , N)]
