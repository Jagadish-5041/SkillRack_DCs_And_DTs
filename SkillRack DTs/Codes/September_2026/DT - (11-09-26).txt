Problem Statement :-

Smallest Numerator And Largest Denominator in DT (Daily Test) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

N = int(input().strip())

numerator_values_list = []

denominator_values_list = []

[((lambda numerator_value , denominator_value : [numerator_values_list.append(numerator_value) , denominator_values_list.append(denominator_value)]) (*map(int , S.split("/")))) for S in input().strip().split()]

smallest_numerator_value = min(numerator_values_list)

largest_denominator_value = max(denominator_values_list)

print(smallest_numerator_value , largest_denominator_value)
