Problem Statement :-

Excluded Integer - Sum Of Adjacent Integers in DT (Daily Test) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

N = int(input().strip())

values_list = list(map(int , input().strip().split()))

for index in range(1 , (N - 1)):
    
    if((values_list[(index - 1)] + values_list[(index + 1)]) != values_list[index]):
        
        print((values_list[index] - values_list[(index - 1)]))
        
        quit()

print((values_list[(N - 1)] - values_list[(N - 2)]))
