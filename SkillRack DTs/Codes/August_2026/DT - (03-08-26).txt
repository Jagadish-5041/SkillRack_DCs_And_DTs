Problem Statement :-

Count Of Common Words in DT (Daily Test) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

words_set_1 = set(input().strip().split())

words_set_2 = set(input().strip().split())

common_words_count = sum(1 for word_string in (words_set_1 | words_set_2) if((word_string in words_set_1) and (word_string in words_set_2)))

print(common_words_count)
