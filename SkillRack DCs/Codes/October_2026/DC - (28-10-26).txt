Problem Statement :-

Remove First K Rows And Columns in DC (Daily Challenge) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

N = int(input().strip())

matrix = [list(map(int , input().strip().split())) for i in range(0 , N)]

K = int(input().strip())

[print(*[matrix[i][j] for j in range(K , N)]) for i in range(K , N)]
