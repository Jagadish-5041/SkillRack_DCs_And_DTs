Problem Statement :-

Reverse Middle Words In S in DC (Daily Challenge) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

words_list = input().strip().split()

N = len(words_list)

answer_list = [(word_string) if((index == 0) or (index == (N - 1))) else (word_string[::-1]) for index , word_string in enumerate(words_list)]

print(*answer_list)
