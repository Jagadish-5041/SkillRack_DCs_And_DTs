Problem Statement :-

Sum Of Multiples Of X From M To N in DC (Daily Challenge) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

M , N , X = map(int , input().strip().split())

if(X > N):
    print(-1)
    quit()

sum_value = sum(number for number in range(M , (N + 1)) if((number % X) == 0))

print((0) if(sum_value == 0) else (sum_value))
