Problem Statement :-

Matrix Non-Border Elements Sum in DC (Daily Challenge) in SkillRack in Python - Programming

Code :-

def is_border_element(i , j , R , C):
    
    return ((i == 0) or (i == (R - 1)) or (j == 0) or (j == (C - 1)))

R , C = map(int , input().strip().split())

matrix = [list(map(int , input().strip().split())) for i in range(0 , R)]

non_border_elements_sum = sum(matrix[i][j] for i in range(0 , R) for j in range(0 , C) if(not is_border_element(i , j , R , C)))

print(non_border_elements_sum)
